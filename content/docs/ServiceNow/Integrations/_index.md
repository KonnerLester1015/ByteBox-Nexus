Integrations are a key part of ServiceNow, allowing you to connect your ServiceNow instance with other systems and applications. Integrations can be used for a variety of purposes, such as provisioning users, synchronizing data, and automating workflows. In this section, you will find documentation on how to set up and manage integrations with ServiceNow.
